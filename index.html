Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about commercial fire alarm installation and service.

What does commercial fire alarm installation include?

It includes a site assessment, system design, equipment supply, wiring, installation of detectors, pull stations and notification devices, programming, testing and training for your staff.

How long does a commercial fire alarm installation take?

Timelines depend on building size, layout and whether it is new construction or an upgrade. After a site visit we give you a clear schedule and work to limit disruption to your business.

How often does a commercial fire alarm system need to be inspected?

Commercial systems need regular inspection and testing in line with NFPA 72 and local fire code requirements. We set up a schedule that fits your building and keeps your records ready for the fire marshal.

Can you upgrade or replace an outdated fire alarm system?

Yes. We evaluate your existing panel and devices, then upgrade or replace what is outdated so the system meets current code and works reliably.

Do you offer fire alarm monitoring?

Yes. Monitoring connects your system to a central station so alarms and trouble signals are acted on around the clock.
